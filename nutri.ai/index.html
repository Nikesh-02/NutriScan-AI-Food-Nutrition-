Our Technology

NutriScan AI combines state-of-the-art computer vision models with a comprehensive nutritional database to provide accurate food analysis. Our AI has been trained on thousands of food images to recognize even complex dishes.

Key Features:

  • Recognizes over 10,000 different food items
  • Estimates portion sizes with 85% accuracy
  • Provides data on 50+ nutrients
  • Works with mixed dishes and cuisines from around the world
  • Continuously improves with user feedback

About NutriScan AI

Our Mission

NutriScan AI was founded with a simple mission: to make nutrition tracking accessible and effortless for everyone. We believe that understanding what's in your food should be as simple as taking a picture.

Our team of nutritionists, data scientists, and software engineers have worked together to create an AI-powered platform that eliminates the guesswork from healthy eating.
